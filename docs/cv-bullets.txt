Built a LangChain baseline and two LangGraph pipelines, corrective RAG and self RAG, over the LangGraph docs, traced in LangSmith and compared on one 50 question benchmark.
Measured correct answers of 38, 37 and 36 of 42 for baseline, corrective and self RAG, a difference under the 3 question noise margin, so neither agentic pipeline measurably beat the baseline.
Scored answers with a rubric judge and RAGAS, and found the agentic pipelines used about 2 and 3 times the tokens per question: 4,577 and 6,847 against 2,334 for the baseline.
